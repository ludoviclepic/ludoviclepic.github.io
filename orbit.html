Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Orbit ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using Orbit, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Orbit keeps your best score, best planet count, number of runs, selected ship colour, and your sound/haptics settings in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Orbit shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when prompted by iOS, and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV)
  • IP address and coarse, non-precise location derived from it
  • Device information: model, OS version, language, screen size, app version
  • Ad interaction data: impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can review ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. Your Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

On first launch, Orbit shows a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → "Manage ad preferences" inside the App. Refusing does not affect gameplay.

4. App Tracking Transparency

Where required, Orbit asks for permission to track through Apple's standard prompt. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads shown to you are non-personalised.

5. No Accounts, No Analytics

Orbit has no user accounts and does not use analytics or crash-reporting services beyond what Apple provides.

6. Children's Privacy

Orbit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
