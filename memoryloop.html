MemoryLoop – Support

See a random number, let it hide, and recall it after 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days. MemoryLoop is a long-term memory game for iPhone and iPad.

Need help? We're happy to assist you with any questions or issues you have with MemoryLoop.

Contact us at: ludo.devapp@gmail.com

When can I recall my number?

Open the Challenges tab. A challenge becomes ready once its delay has passed, and a reminder is sent if notifications are allowed. Reminders never show the number.

How does ranking work?

Only an exact first attempt made soon after the challenge becomes ready is ranked. A second attempt is always allowed but never ranked. Changing the device clock while a challenge is pending makes it unranked. See Settings → How it works in the app.

My scores do not appear in Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center. Scores earned while offline or signed out are kept on the device and sent when Game Center becomes available.

Ads and privacy choices

MemoryLoop shows a single banner on menu screens, never while you memorize or recall. You can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy and ads → Privacy choices (where it applies) and tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To report an inappropriate ad, use Settings → Report an ad or email us.

Deleting my data

Your challenges and results are stored only on your device. Delete a challenge from its detail screen, remove results from Records → History, or delete the app to remove all of its data.
