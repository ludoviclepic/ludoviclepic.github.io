Orbit for Android

Privacy policy

You can play Orbit without signing in. Preferences and your local record are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that local data.

When the leaderboard is enabled and you are signed in to Google Play Games, Orbit sends your best score to the service. Google associates the score with your Play Games profile. Your player name, avatar and score may appear to other players according to your Play Games settings. You can manage your profile, visibility and Play Games data in Google Play Games. Removing the local copy of Orbit does not remove scores stored by Google. See Google Play Games help.

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics and interactions for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Your answers and saved progress are not sent to AdMob. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google’s consent form appears where required. You can reopen advertising preferences via Info when available. Gameplay is available independently of personalized ads.

If you contact support, your email and message are used to respond to you. For privacy questions or requests, contact ludo.devapp@gmail.com.
